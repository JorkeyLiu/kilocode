# P4.4-G3 remote/status private carrier — first single-operation Gate C/D runtime batch

- **Owner:** `KiloSessions` module-closure state (`packages/opencode/src/kilo-sessions/kilo-sessions.ts`
  `remoteStatus()`) is the single authority for process-global `{enabled, connected}`. `kilo serve`
  fd3/fd4 carrier (`packages/opencode/src/kilocode/server/fd-carrier.ts`) and extension
  `ServerManager`/`KiloConnectionService`/`ServePrivatePeer`/`RemoteStatusService` are the only carrier
  consumers. No second remote state store, no second `AppLayer`.
- **Scope:** exactly `remote/status`. `path.get`, `experimental/session/list`, `command/list`,
  `config/warnings`, `session/abort`, `remote.enable`/`remote.disable`, and `RemoteStatusChanged`
  event-stream parity are out of scope. SDK `client.remote.status` (`GET /remote/status`) remains the
  sole user-visible authority; the private result is diagnostics-only parity observation, never a
  source of state, cutover, or fallback.
- **Semantics:** `enabled`/`connected` are process-global. Directory/workspace are routing identity
  only; cross-directory equality of the two booleans is expected, not a scope isolation claim and not
  a divergence.
